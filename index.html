Hi, I'm Arslan!

I work as a Product Owner in Berlin and since October 2026 I'm a student in the master's program Business Intelligence & Process Management (BIPM) at HWR Berlin.

About me

From 2014 to 2020 I studied biology and chemistry at Freie Universität Berlin because I wanted to become a teacher. During that time I also worked in labs. After some years I noticed that I was more interested in business and in how companies decide which products they build. So in 2020 I switched to Business Administration at HTW Berlin.

In 2021 I started working next to my studies as a Product Marketing Consultant at the content agency Suxeedo. In 2022 I joined PwC as a Product Owner and worked on a Workday add-on. Since 2024 I'm a Product Owner at Scheer IMC. I work remotely on the IMC Learning Suite and I'm responsible for dashboards, apps, generative AI and third-party integrations. I'm also a Certified Scrum Product Owner.

In my job many decisions should be based on data, but often I need other people to get the data or analyse it for me. That's why I started BIPM. I want to learn how to work with Python and SQL myself and to understand the processes behind the numbers.

I speak German, English and Urdu/Hindi.
